Supported scope

Camera only: position, orientation and full-frame focal length, sampled at 24 fps. Recorded motion is used when available; otherwise the captured pose is transferred.

Three (x, y, z) becomes Blender (x, −z, y). One unit is one meter. Output framing is 16:9 with a 36 mm sensor width.

All geometry, including the subject, materials, lighting, fog, device signals and physics are omitted. This is not a lossless round trip.

Target · disposable Blender project

Creates a uniquely named scene and leaves existing scenes intact. Restores the active scene. Previs does not save the .blend file: save it in Blender to retain the new scene after Blender restarts.
